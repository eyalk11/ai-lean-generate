# Adding two on either side

## Statement

For every natural number `n`, adding two to `n` gives the same result as
adding `n` to two:

`n + 2 = 2 + n`.

## Human proof

Addition of natural numbers does not depend on the order of its arguments.
Applying that fact to `n` and `2` gives the claim directly.

The formal result should quantify explicitly over `n : Nat` and use no
unproved assumptions.
